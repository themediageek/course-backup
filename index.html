Course backup

This site holds read-only copies of Canvas courses, so students can keep reading course materials while Canvas is unavailable.

Students

Use the link and password your instructor sent you. Each course has its own link, and this page does not list them.

If the link or the password does not work, write to your instructor.

Faculty

Load your Canvas course export, preview it, and make it live for your students. The export stays on your computer, and only a password-protected copy goes online.
